04. Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO)

State-of-the-art policy safety margins via bounded updates.

Standard Policy Gradient methods can occasionally collapse during training. If a parameter update shifts network probabilities too drastically, the agent can fall into an unrecoverable state of terrible choices, completely ruining performance.

PPO introduces a massive upgrade: a clipped surrogate objective function. It tracks a probability ratio \(r_t(\theta)\) between the new policy and the old policy, forcing updates to stay inside a tight safety window (typically between \(0.8\) and \(1.2\)).

L^CLIP(θ) = E [ min( r_t(θ) * A_t, clip(r_t(θ), 1-ε, 1+ε) * A_t ) ]

Parameter Glossary:

  • \(r_t(\theta)\): Probability Ratio. Measures how much more likely an action is under the new policy vs old policy.
  • \(A_t\): Advantage function. Tracks if an action performed better or worse than the baseline average.
  • \(\epsilon\) (Clip bound): Hyperparameter (usually 0.2) preventing large destructive update spikes.
