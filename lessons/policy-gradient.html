03. Policy Gradients

Optimizing actions directly by shifting probability distributions.

Unlike DQN, which estimates how "good" a state-action pair is, Policy Gradient methods model the policy function \(\pi_\theta(a|s)\) directly. The neural network inputs a state and directly outputs a probability distribution over possible actions.

The core objective is to adjust network weights \(\theta\) to increase the probability of actions that lead to good outcomes, and decrease the probability of actions that lead to poor outcomes.

The standard baseline architecture is the REINFORCE algorithm, driven by the Policy Gradient Theorem objective function:

∇θ J(θ) = E [ ∇θ log πθ(a|s) * G_t ]

Parameter Glossary:

  • \(\pi_\theta(a|s)\): Policy network outputting the probability of action \(a\) given state \(s\).
  • \(G_t\): The total discounted return from time step \(t\) until the end of the episode.
  • Log Derivative trick: Converts the complex derivative of an expectation into an easily computable sample gradient.
